Imagined proposition for a "2027 Design Day" event with the theme "Authorship and Artificial Intelligence"

![flyer](https://images.tiagoferreira.work/autoria/autoria-desdobravel-final.png)

With authors, workshops, lectures and debates. Physical and digital (social media) material.

![gif redes](https://images.tiagoferreira.work/autoria/autoria-site-faculdade.gif)

The visual identity comes from "Glaze", an AI database poisoning tool created to protect the authors. The noise used as the main graphical base of the event is extracted from the filter applied by "Glaze" and used in addition to multiple bright colors.
